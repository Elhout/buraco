## English

**Who we are.** Buraco Score is operated by [Operator]. Contact: [contact email].

**What we collect and why**
| Data | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Username and password (the password is stored only as a salted hash) | To sign you in |
| Display name, optional avatar, language | To show you to your club and in your language |
| Your PIN (stored only as a salted bcrypt hash) | To confirm game actions; we cannot read it |
| Club membership and roles | To share games only within your club |
| Game data: seats, round scores, bonuses, confirmations, audit log, tournament entries | The core service: live scores, history and stats |
| Device push token, platform, whether the app is open on a game | To send game notifications and avoid duplicates |
| Crash reports (device model, OS, app version, stack traces; no game content) | To fix crashes (Sentry) |

We do not sell data, show ads, or track you across apps.

**Who can see it.** Members of the same club see each other's names, games and stats. Nothing
is visible across clubs. Service providers process data on our behalf: Supabase (database and
authentication, [hosting region]), Expo (push delivery), Apple/Google (push), Sentry (crash
reports).

**Retention.** Game history is kept while your account exists so club records stay correct.
Push tokens are deleted when you sign out or a device stops accepting notifications.

**Your choices.** Change your name, language and notification settings in Profile. Leave a club
at any time. To delete your account, use Profile → Delete account (or email [contact email]); your name is removed and your past
games are kept anonymized so other members' records stay consistent.

**Children.** The app is not directed to children under 13.

**Changes.** We will post updates here and in the app.

**من يمكنه رؤيتها.** يرى أعضاء النادي نفسه أسماء بعضهم ومبارياتهم وإحصاءاتهم، ولا يظهر شيء خارج النادي. يعالج مزوّدو الخدمة البيانات نيابةً عنا: Supabase (قاعدة البيانات والمصادقة، [hosting region])، وExpo (إرسال الإشعارات)، وApple/Google (الإشعارات)، وSentry (تقارير الأعطال).

**مدة الاحتفاظ.** يُحتفظ بسجل المباريات ما دام حسابك قائمًا حتى تبقى سجلات النادي صحيحة. تُحذف رموز الإشعارات عند تسجيل الخروج أو عندما يتوقف الجهاز عن استقبالها.

**خياراتك.** غيّر اسمك ولغتك وإعدادات الإشعارات من الملف الشخصي، ويمكنك مغادرة أي نادٍ في أي وقت. لحذف حسابك استخدم الملف الشخصي ← حذف الحساب (أو راسلنا على [contact email])؛ يُزال اسمك وتُحفظ مبارياتك السابقة دون هوية حتى تبقى سجلات الأعضاء الآخرين متسقة.
